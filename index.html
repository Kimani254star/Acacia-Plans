Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters.

Where does the data come from?

It comes from your Acacia Books company, so employee and payroll records stay in one place.

Can I produce payslips?

Yes. Payslips can be produced for each employee.

Does it handle statutory deductions?

Yes. There is a Statutory Deductions area and payroll reports.

Is my data kept per company?

Yes. Each company has its own separate data, and everyone in that company sees the same records.
